# Olist Marketing Funnel — Real Closed Deals Insights

This analysis is based on 842 real, anonymized closed deals from the Olist Marketing Funnel dataset (Brazilian e-commerce marketplace, 2017-2018), used here as reference data for lead-scoring calibration.

## Deal Volume
Total closed deals analyzed: 842.

## Revenue Declaration
Only 45 out of 842 businesses (about 5.3%) declared their monthly revenue at the time of closing. Among those who did, the average declared monthly revenue was approximately $1,372,977.91, with the highest single declaration reaching $50,000,000. This wide variance shows that most closed deals come from businesses that do not disclose revenue upfront, so revenue alone is a weak predictor of deal likelihood; other signals like lead type and business segment matter more.

## Top Business Segments (by closed deal count)
1. Home decor — 105 deals
2. Health & beauty — 93 deals
3. Car accessories — 77 deals
4. Household utilities — 71 deals
5. Construction, tools, house & garden — 69 deals

## Lead Type Distribution
- Online medium: 332 deals
- Online big: 126 deals
- Industry: 123 deals
- Offline: 104 deals
- Online small: 77 deals

Online-medium leads convert into closed deals far more often than any other type, suggesting mid-sized digital inbound leads are the strongest source for this kind of business.

## Business Type Breakdown
- Reseller: 587 deals (about 70%)
- Manufacturer: 242 deals (about 29%)
- Other: 3 deals

Resellers make up the large majority of closed deals compared to manufacturers.
