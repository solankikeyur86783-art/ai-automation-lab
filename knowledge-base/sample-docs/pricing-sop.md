# Keyur AI Automation Lab — Pricing & Engagement SOP

## Pricing Tiers
1. Starter Automation (single workflow): fixed price, includes one n8n workflow such as lead intake or notification automation, delivered within 5 business days.
2. Growth Package (multi-workflow + AI): includes lead qualification with AI scoring, CRM integration, and basic dashboard. Delivered within 2 weeks.
3. Enterprise RAG System: full company knowledge assistant with document ingestion, vector search, custom dashboard, and human approval workflows. Timeline depends on scope, typically 3-4 weeks.

## Standard Operating Procedure for New Client Onboarding
Step 1: Discovery call to understand the client's current process and pain points.
Step 2: Written scope of work with clear deliverables and timeline.
Step 3: Access setup — client provides necessary API keys, form details, or document access.
Step 4: Build phase with weekly progress updates.
Step 5: User acceptance testing (UAT) period of 3-5 days before going live.
Step 6: Handover with documentation, architecture diagram, and a short training session.

## Support Policy
All projects include 14 days of free bug-fix support after delivery. Ongoing maintenance and monitoring is available as a separate monthly retainer.

## Data Handling
Client data is never used to train external AI models. All API keys and credentials are stored securely and never shared across client projects.
