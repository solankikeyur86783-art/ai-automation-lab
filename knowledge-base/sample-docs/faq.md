# Keyur AI Automation Lab — Frequently Asked Questions

## What services do you offer?
We offer AI-powered business automation using n8n, AI agents, and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). Our core services include lead qualification, customer support automation, document processing, and custom AI knowledge assistants for companies.

## How does lead qualification work?
When a lead submits a form, our system automatically saves it to the database, uses an AI model to score the lead from 1-100, categorizes it as hot, warm, or cold, and sends real-time alerts for hot leads via Discord or Slack.

## What is RAG and why does it matter?
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) lets an AI assistant answer questions using your company's own documents instead of guessing. It retrieves relevant text chunks from a vector database and grounds every answer in your actual data, reducing hallucination.

## Do you offer a free trial?
We offer a free consultation call to understand your automation needs before any commitment. Implementation timelines depend on project scope.

## What is your typical project turnaround time?
A basic lead automation workflow typically takes 3-5 business days. A full RAG knowledge assistant with a custom dashboard typically takes 2-3 weeks depending on document volume and integrations required.

## What technologies do you use?
We use n8n for workflow automation, Python and FastAPI for backend services, Qdrant as a vector database, PostgreSQL for structured data, LangGraph for AI agents, and Next.js for dashboards.
